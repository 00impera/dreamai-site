How it works

Three steps, no onboarding call required.

  • Describe what you need

    Write a short brief — audience, tone, length. The more specific, the closer the first draft.

  • Review and refine

    Edit inline or ask for a rewrite of any section. Nothing publishes until you approve it.

  • Export or publish

    Download the file or push it directly to your site. Your account keeps the full history.
